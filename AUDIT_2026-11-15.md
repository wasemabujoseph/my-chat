# FMHYPlay / AnantPlay Arabic Sources: web audit

- **Audit date:** 2026-11-15
- **Scope:** all 34 site entries originally in the source list, after the previous ArabSeed fix.
- **Methods:** open URLs through a remote text/browser fetcher, inspect redirects, check searchable catalogs, and cross-check selected indexed pages. No app playback testing was possible.
- **Limitations:** A 403/timeout/502 result can come from anti-bot or geographic restrictions; it does NOT prove a site is broken in AnantPlay. A homepage being accessible does NOT prove videos will play, nor that access is secure or licensed.

## Confirmed changes pushed
1. `Fasel HD`: `https://www.fasel-hd.co/` → `https://www.fasel-hd.co/movies` (catalog page loaded).
2. `EgyBest`: `https://egybe.st/` → `https://iegybest.cimawbas.tv/` (user-provided host; catalog page loaded and redirect observed).
3. `ShoFree`: `https://shoffree.cc/` → `https://shoffree.cc/explore` (bypasses welcome page).
4. Removed `AnimeArabs` as it is an anime **news** website, not a streaming catalog. Previous address: `https://animearabs.com/`.

## Installation / refresh
Open the existing source in **Settings → Sources**; use **Settings → Sites → Refresh sources** or allow periodic refresh. AnantPlay normally refreshes added source files every 12 hours. It caches data locally.

## Safety
Do not enter passwords into unexpected redirects, download third-party APKs, or assume a media site's ownership or content rights without checking. Avoid attempts to bypass authorization, paywalls, or DRM.
